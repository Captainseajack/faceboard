## 1.0.0

Initial public release:

- Horizontal group bar, connected photo chains, and editable nested junctions.

- Drag photos between chains and the unplaced photo tray.
- Resize one photo in Details, or all photos with the Photo size control.
- Scroll, pan, fit the canvas, and zoom into individual entries.
- Local saving, undo/redo, and self-contained JSON backups.
- Standalone offline HTML file with no installation or dependencies.
- Optional Cash App tips for Jack.
- AI-generation disclosure and MIT licensing.
